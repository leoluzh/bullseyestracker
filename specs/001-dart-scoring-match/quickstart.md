# Quickstart: Dart Scoring & Match Tracking

Validation guide proving the feature works end-to-end once implemented. Not an implementation
guide — see `tasks.md` (generated by `/speckit-tasks`) for build-out steps.

## Prerequisites

- Physical Android device (emulator camera feed is not representative for CV validation),
  minSdk 26+, with rear camera.
- A standard, regulation-pattern dartboard, indoor lighting, camera roughly perpendicular to
  the board face at a distance where the whole board fills most of the frame.
- App built and installed from the `app` module (`./gradlew :app:installDebug` once the
  module exists).

## Scenario 1 — Board calibration (validates FR-001, contracts/cv-engine-contract.md)

1. Open the app, point the camera at an empty dartboard (no darts thrown).
2. Expect: the app indicates the board was found and overlays the detected ring/sector
   geometry roughly matching the physical board.
3. Negative case: point the camera at a blank wall. Expect: the app reports no board found
   (edge case in spec.md), not a wrong/garbage calibration.

## Scenario 2 — Live-camera auto-scoring (validates User Story 1, FR-002/004/005)

1. With the board calibrated (Scenario 1), throw a dart.
2. Expect: within the performance budget (constitution Principle IV, <200ms), the app shows
   the dart's position and score overlaid on the live view.
3. Throw 2 more darts. Expect: all 3 appear with individual scores.
4. Tap one overlaid detection and change its sector/ring. Expect: the score updates
   immediately and is reflected in the eventual turn total (validates FR-006).

## Scenario 3 — Photo-based auto-scoring (validates User Story 2, FR-003)

1. With 3 darts already in the board, switch to photo mode and take one photo.
2. Expect: the app detects all 3 darts in the still image and shows the same overlay/scoring
   UI as the live-camera flow, with the same correction capability.

## Scenario 4 — 501 match (validates User Story 3, FR-007/008/009/010)

1. Start a new match, choose 501, add 2 players.
2. Expect: both players start at 501; app shows whose turn it is.
3. Complete a turn via Scenario 2 or 3's flow, confirm it. Expect: the active player's
   remaining score decreases by the confirmed turn total, and turn advances to the next player.
4. Drive a player to a turn total that would take them below 0 (e.g., manually correct throws
   to force it). Expect: the turn is marked a bust, their score reverts, turn advances.
5. Drive a player to exactly 0 with a double-ring final dart. Expect: match ends, that player
   is declared winner.

## Scenario 5 — Cricket match (validates User Story 4)

1. Start a new match, choose Cricket, add 2 players.
2. Land 3 marks on the same number for one player across turns (e.g., three 20s). Expect: that
   number shows as closed for that player.
3. With the number closed for player A but open for player B, have player A hit it again.
   Expect: player A's points increase by that number's value.
4. Close all numbers (15-20 + bull) for a player who also leads on points among players with
   that state. Expect: match ends, that player is declared winner.

## Scenario 6 — Offline operation (validates FR-013, constitution Principle I)

1. Enable airplane mode on the device.
2. Repeat Scenario 2 and Scenario 4 (steps 1-3). Expect: full functionality, no errors related
   to connectivity.

## Scenario 7 — Match resume (validates FR-011)

1. Mid-match (any mode), force-close the app.
2. Reopen it. Expect: the in-progress match resumes with correct player scores/marks and the
   correct current turn — no data loss.

## Out of scope for this quickstart

- Detection accuracy benchmarking against SC-002's 90% target — that requires a curated test
  image set and is tracked as an implementation/testing task, not a manual quickstart step.
- Match history browsing (FR-014) — straightforward list/detail view, not a distinct
  validation risk worth a dedicated scenario here.
